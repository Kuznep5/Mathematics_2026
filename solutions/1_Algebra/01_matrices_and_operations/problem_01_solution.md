# Matrices and Basic Operations

## Exercise 1. Matrix Size and Entries

Given the matrices:

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}
2&-1&3\\
0&4&5
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
B=\begin{pmatrix}
1&0\\
-2&3\\
4&1
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

### 1. Matrix sizes

Matrix \(A\) has 2 rows and 3 columns. Therefore, its size is:

$$
\boxed{2\times3}
$$

Matrix \(B\) has 3 rows and 2 columns. Therefore, its size is:

$$
\boxed{3\times2}
$$

### 2. Matrix entries

The first index represents the row, and the second index represents the column.

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{a_{12}=-1}
$$

$$
\boxed{a_{23}=5}
$$

$$
\boxed{b_{21}=-2}
$$

$$
\boxed{b_{32}=1}
$$

### 3. Row and column vectors

The second row of matrix \(A\) is:

$$
\boxed{\begin{pmatrix}0&4&5\end{pmatrix}}
$$

The first column of matrix \(B\) is:

$$
\boxed{\begin{pmatrix}1\\-2\\4\end{pmatrix}}
$$

### Conclusion

Matrix \(A\) has dimensions \(2\times3\), and matrix \(B\) has dimensions \(3\times2\). Matrix entries are identified by their row and column indices. Rows are written horizontally, while columns are written vertically.
