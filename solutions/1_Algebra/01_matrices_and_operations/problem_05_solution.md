## Exercise 5 — Matrix-Vector Multiplication

Given:

$$
A =
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & -1 \\
1 & 3
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
x =
\begin{pmatrix}
4 \\
2
\end{pmatrix}
$$

Calculate \(Ax\):

$$
Ax =
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & -1 \\
1 & 3
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}
4 \\
2
\end{pmatrix}
$$

Multiply each row of the matrix by the vector:

$$
Ax =
\begin{pmatrix}
2\cdot4 + (-1)\cdot2 \\
1\cdot4 + 3\cdot2
\end{pmatrix}
$$

Calculate:

$$
Ax =
\begin{pmatrix}
8 - 2 \\
4 + 6
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
6 \\
10
\end{pmatrix}
$$

**Final answer:**

$$
\boxed{
Ax =
\begin{pmatrix}
6 \\
10
\end{pmatrix}
}
$$
