## Exercise 7 — Scalar Multiplication

Given:

$$
A =
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 1 \\
0 & 2
\end{pmatrix}
$$

For example, calculate \(2A\):

$$
2A =
2\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 1 \\
0 & 2
\end{pmatrix}
$$

Multiply every element by 2:

$$
2A =
\begin{pmatrix}
2\cdot2 & 2\cdot1 \\
2\cdot0 & 2\cdot2
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
4 & 2 \\
0 & 4
\end{pmatrix}
$$

**Final answer:**

$$
\boxed{
2A =
\begin{pmatrix}
4 & 2 \\
0 & 4
\end{pmatrix}
}
$$
