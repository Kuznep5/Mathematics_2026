## Exercise 10 — Matrix Multiplication

Given:

$$
A =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 2 & 0 \\
0 & 1 & 1
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
B =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 2 \\
-1 & 0 \\
3 & 1
\end{pmatrix}
$$

Calculate \(AB\):

$$
AB =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 2 & 0 \\
0 & 1 & 1
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 2 \\
-1 & 0 \\
3 & 1
\end{pmatrix}
$$

Calculate each element:

$$
AB =
\begin{pmatrix}
1(1)+2(-1)+0(3) & 1(2)+2(0)+0(1) \\
0(1)+1(-1)+1(3) & 0(2)+1(0)+1(1)
\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
AB =
\begin{pmatrix}
-1 & 2 \\
2 & 1
\end{pmatrix}
$$

**Final answer:**

$$
\boxed{
AB =
\begin{pmatrix}
-1 & 2 \\
2 & 1
\end{pmatrix}
}
$$
