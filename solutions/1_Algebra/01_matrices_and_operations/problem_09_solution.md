## Exercise 9 — Matrix Transformations

Given:

$$
S =
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 0 \\
0 & 1
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
R =
\begin{pmatrix}
0 & -1 \\
1 & 0
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
x =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 \\
2
\end{pmatrix}
$$

### Apply transformation S

$$
Sx =
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 0 \\
0 & 1
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}
1 \\
2
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
2\cdot1+0\cdot2 \\
0\cdot1+1\cdot2
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
2 \\
2
\end{pmatrix}
$$

### Apply transformation R

$$
Rx =
\begin{pmatrix}
0 & -1 \\
1 & 0
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}
1 \\
2
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
0\cdot1+(-1)\cdot2 \\
1\cdot1+0\cdot2
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
-2 \\
1
\end{pmatrix}
$$

**Final answers:**

$$
\boxed{Sx=\begin{pmatrix}2\\2\end{pmatrix}}
\qquad
\boxed{Rx=\begin{pmatrix}-2\\1\end{pmatrix}}
$$
