## Exercise 6 — Matrix Multiplication

Given:

$$
A =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 2 & 3 \\
4 & 5 & 6
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
B =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 \\
2 & 1 \\
-1 & 3
\end{pmatrix}
$$

Calculate \(AB\):

$$
AB =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 2 & 3 \\
4 & 5 & 6
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 \\
2 & 1 \\
-1 & 3
\end{pmatrix}
$$

Calculate each element:

$$
AB =
\begin{pmatrix}
1(1)+2(2)+3(-1) & 1(0)+2(1)+3(3) \\
4(1)+5(2)+6(-1) & 4(0)+5(1)+6(3)
\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
AB =
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 11 \\
8 & 23
\end{pmatrix}
$$

**Final answer:**

$$
\boxed{
AB =
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 11 \\
8 & 23
\end{pmatrix}
}
$$
