## Exercise 2. Addition and Scalar Multiplication

Given:

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}
1&2\\
-1&3
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
B=\begin{pmatrix}
4&-2\\
0&5
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

### 1. Matrix Addition

To calculate \(A+B\), add the corresponding entries of both matrices.

$$
A+B=
\begin{pmatrix}
1+4&2+(-2)\\
-1+0&3+5
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Therefore,

$$
\boxed{
A+B=
\begin{pmatrix}
5&0\\
-1&8
\end{pmatrix}
}
$$

### 2. Matrix Subtraction

To calculate \(A-B\), subtract the corresponding entries of matrix \(B\) from matrix \(A\).

$$
A-B=
\begin{pmatrix}
1-4&2-(-2)\\
-1-0&3-5
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Therefore,

$$
\boxed{
A-B=
\begin{pmatrix}
-3&4\\
-1&-2
\end{pmatrix}
}
$$

### 3. Scalar Multiplication

First, multiply every entry of \(A\) by 3:

$$
3A=
\begin{pmatrix}
3&6\\
-3&9
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Next, multiply every entry of \(B\) by 2:

$$
2B=
\begin{pmatrix}
8&-4\\
0&10
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Now subtract the resulting matrices:

$$
3A-2B=
\begin{pmatrix}
3-8&6-(-4)\\
-3-0&9-10
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Therefore,

$$
\boxed{
3A-2B=
\begin{pmatrix}
-5&10\\
-3&-1
\end{pmatrix}
}
$$

### Explanation

Matrix addition and subtraction are possible only when both matrices have the same dimensions. The corresponding entries are added or subtracted individually.

Scalar multiplication means multiplying every entry of a matrix by the same number.

Since both \(A\) and \(B\) are \(2\times2\) matrices, all the required operations are defined.
