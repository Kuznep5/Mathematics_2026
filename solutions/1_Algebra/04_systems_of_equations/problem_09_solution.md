### Exercise 9. A System from a Sum Condition
 
**Problem.** Find $x,y$ with $x+y=10$ and $2x+5y=32$.
 
**Solution.**
 
$$
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 1 & \mid & 10 \\
2 & 5 & \mid & 32 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$
 
Apply $R_2\leftarrow R_2-2R_1$:
 
$$
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 1 & \mid & 10 \\
0 & 3 & \mid & 12 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$
 
Hence $y=4$ and $x=10-4=6$.
 
**Check.** $6+4=10$ and $2\cdot6+5\cdot4=32$.
 
**Geometric explanation.** Each equation is a line in the plane. Their slopes are $-1$ and $-\tfrac25$, which are different, so the lines are not parallel and meet in exactly one point.
 
**Answer.** $(x,y)=(6,4)$.
