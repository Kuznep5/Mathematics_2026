### Exercise 6. Parameter and Invertibility
 
$$
A(t)=
\begin{pmatrix}
t & 1 \\
2 & t \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$
 
The determinant is
 
$$
\det A(t) = t\cdot t-1\cdot 2 = t^2-2.
$$
 
The matrix is not invertible exactly when $\det A(t)=0$, that is, when $t^2=2$. Hence
 
$$
t=\sqrt{2}, \qquad t=-\sqrt{2}.
$$
 
For every other value of $t$ the determinant is nonzero and $A(t)$ is invertible. At $t=\pm\sqrt2$ the rows are proportional, so the matrix is singular.
